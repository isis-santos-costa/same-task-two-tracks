The Data Profiling reviewed 60 customer records to evaluate overall completeness and quality. The assessment identified missing values in: customer_id. The evaluation shown in Details breaks down across three core field classifications: high-cardinality identifiers, low-cardinality categories, and numerical metrics.

For high-cardinality fields (customer_id, email, state), uniqueness checks verify entity separation and identifier integrity. Primary identifiers (IDs) are expected to be 100% unique. Business identifiers may tolerate a limited % of reuse, e.g., phone number. There are also high-cardinality fields for which uniqueness is expected to be lower, like state. For low-cardinality fields (loyalty_tier, account_active), the analysis inspects categorical consistency, distribution, and balance. For numerical metrics (age, purchase_amount, signup_date), the validation monitors the average and the core distribution for the field, with q0, q100 being the min/max values, q50 the median, and q25, q75 the boundaries of the interquartile range. This set of metrics enables the inspection of value ranges, distribution boundaries, and anomaly detection to flag logical errors such as out-of-bound values or invalid dates.
